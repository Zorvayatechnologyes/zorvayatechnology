04

The five laws of the training arc

Every hero's montage obeys the same rules. Break these and no amount of dramatic music saves you.

LAW 01

Progressive overload

Each week, add a rep or a little weight. The villain (your previous self) must keep getting stronger too.

LAW 02

Show up anyway

A mediocre session beats a skipped one. Plot armor doesn't exist; consistency is the only cheat code.

LAW 03

Eat like it matters

Muscle is built at the table, revealed in the gym. Hit your protein before you worry about supplements.

LAW 04

Sleep 7–9 hours

This is when the upgrade montage actually plays. Cut sleep and you cut your gains.

LAW 05

Form before weight

Master the technique first — an injury is the worst possible filler episode. Film your sets and check.

05

The recovery kitchen

Four simple rules that cover 90% of anime-hero eating. No exotic supplements, no eating your bodyweight in rice unless you want to.

Donburi Rule

Protein every meal

Eggs, paneer, chicken, fish, dal, whey, tofu, curd. Anchor every plate with a palm-to-two of protein first.

Bento Rule

Mostly whole foods

80% home-cooked: grains, pulses, vegetables, fruit, dairy. The other 20% keeps you sane — enjoy the festival episode.

Ramen Rule

Carbs around training

Put your rice, roti, oats or potatoes close to workouts. Carbs are not the enemy; they're your ki blast.

Onigiri Rule

Easy calories win

Struggling to grow? Milk, nuts, ghee, peanut butter, bananas. Struggling to lean out? More veggies and protein, less liquid calories.
